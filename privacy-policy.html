Virelix Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Virelix (the “App”), a video-editing application, may collect, use, and protect information when you use it.

Information the App may process

  • Account information: If you sign in, authentication details and account identifiers may be processed by Firebase Authentication or the configured sign-in provider.
  • Projects and content: Videos, images, audio, project details, or related files that you select may be accessed to provide editing features. Files are processed on your device unless you choose a feature that uploads, syncs, or otherwise sends them to a server.
  • Cloud data: If cloud features are enabled and used, project metadata or files you submit may be processed using Firebase Firestore, Firebase Storage, or backend functions configured for the App.
  • Technical information: The App and its service providers may process technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service, such as app or device information and service logs, depending on the services enabled.

How information is used

Information is used to provide and maintain editing and account features, store or sync content when you request those features, respond to support requests, protect the App from abuse, troubleshoot errors, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support the App, including Google Firebase services when those services are enabled. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or when necessary to protect users, the App, or others.

Files and permissions

The App may request access to media or files you choose to edit or export. Grant only the permissions needed for the features you use. You can manage app permissions in your device settings. Exported files may be saved to a location you select.

Data retention and deletion

Files stored only on your device can generally be removed by deleting them from your device or uninstalling the App, subject to your device’s backup settings. Cloud-stored information, if the App offers cloud storage, may remain until it is deleted through the App or a supported account/data deletion process. Service providers may retain limited records for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Children’s privacy

Virelix is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer using the contact details on the App’s Google Play listing.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You can choose which media to open or upload, manage permissions through your device settings, and stop using the App at any time. Where available, use the App’s account or data deletion controls to request deletion of cloud data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App’s features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL with its updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact email shown on the Virelix Google Play listing.
